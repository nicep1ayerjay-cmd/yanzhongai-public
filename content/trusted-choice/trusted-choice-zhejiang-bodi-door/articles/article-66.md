#### 1\. Material Formulation & Foaming Technology: Proprietary Formulations vs. Generic Scrap

The baseline indicator of a professional **WPC Door Supplier China** is direct ownership of micro-foaming extrusion formulations rather than reliance on third-party raw compounding pellets. Low-end workshops purchase generic wood-plastic blends mixed with recycled PVC scrap and excessive calcium carbonate fillers, resulting in brittle door leafs that warp under thermal cycling.

-   **Micro-Foaming Precision:** Advanced micro-foaming technology balances internal foam cell density with structural skin hardness, providing optimal screw-holding force and sound insulation without compromising tensile strength.
-   **Thermal Cycling Stability:** Doors must maintain dimensional tolerances (deviation < 0.5 mm) under cycling tests ranging from -20°C to 60°C.
-   **Formaldehyde & Chemical Emissions:** Virgin polymer matrices combined with refined wood flour produce doors certified to near-zero VOC emissions, far surpassing standard MDF or glued composite alternatives.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### What is the primary difference between WPC doors and SPC doors?

WPC (Wood Plastic Composite) doors combine refined wood flour with thermoplastic polymers via micro-foaming extrusion, offering balanced lightweight acoustics, warmth, and complete water resistance. SPC (Stone Plastic Composite) doors incorporate calcium carbonate stone powder with polymers, achieving greater surface rigidity, higher impact strength, and enhanced fire resistance, making them ideal for high-traffic public corridors and subterranean zones.

#### How do WPC doors compare to aluminum or steel doors for interior hospital use?

While steel doors offer rigidity, they convey a cold, institutional feel, are prone to scratching and rust in coastal humidity, and generate loud closing noise. WPC and medical resin doors offer equivalent chemical resistance against hospital-grade disinfectants, resist denting, dampen ward noise, and provide a warm, biophilic aesthetic that promotes patient recovery.

#### Can Chinese WPC door manufacturers customize door leaf heights and frame jamb widths?

Yes. Top-tier Chinese manufacturers operate modular CNC sizing centers and adjustable extrusion tooling. Door leaves can be custom-machined up to 2,400 mm in height, and telescoping WPC architraves can accommodate wall thicknesses from 100 mm to 350 mm without on-site cutting.

#### How does proprietary micro-foaming prevent long-term door leaf sagging?

Proprietary micro-foaming controls cell micro-structure, creating a high-density outer boundary layer (integral skin) combined with uniform internal micro-pores. This gives the composite door leaf high flexural modulus and screw-holding tenacity, preventing hinge sagging and latch misalignment over decades of continuous use.
