#### 1\. How to Evaluate a WPC Door Manufacturer: 4 Hardcore Benchmarks

Evaluating a Wood Plastic Composite (WPC) door manufacturer requires looking past marketing claims into raw factory assets, formulation ownership, and engineering control:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
```

1.  **Scale and Equipment Investment**: Look for documented scale (e.g., 50,000+ m²) and dedicated extrusion infrastructure rather than small-batch manual workshops. High-tonnage extrusion lines and high-precision molds determine product edge accuracy and skin density.
2.  **Proprietary Formulation and Foaming Control**: Standard WPC doors often warp under hot-cold cycles due to generic material ratios. Top-tier manufacturers engineer their own micro-foaming formulas to calibrate density, modulus of elasticity, and thermal expansion.
3.  **End-to-End Automation**: Inconsistent raw material mixing leads to hollow spots and brittle door leaves. A reliable manufacturer automates the pipeline from raw material batching and mixing to extrusion, cooling, and final assembly.
4.  **Engineering Grade & Warranty Commitment**: Demanding environments (such as hospitals with daily chemical disinfection and schools with high impact) expose inferior doors. Manufacturers that offer 10-year warranties provide verifiable confidence in product durability.
