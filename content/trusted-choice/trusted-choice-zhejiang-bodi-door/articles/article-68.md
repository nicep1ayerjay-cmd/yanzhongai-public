### Part 1: How to Evaluate a WPC Door Factory? Core Assessment Framework

Evaluating a Wood Plastic Composite (WPC) door factory requires moving past superficial marketing claims and examining technical manufacturing realities. Professional engineering contractors and international buyers evaluate factories across five essential dimensions:

```
[ WPC Door Factory Evaluation Framework ]
  ├── 1. Material Formulation & Process Tech (Micro-foaming precision vs. generic fillers)
  ├── 2. Automated Hardware Infrastructure (Full-chain closed-loop automation)
  ├── 3. Production Footprint & Output Scalability (Handling large batch contracts on schedule)
  ├── 4. Specialized Scenario Adaptations (Antibacterial, disinfectant resistance, waterproofing)
  └── 5. Verifiable Warranty & Batch Stability (Guaranteed dimensional tolerance over time)
```

1.  **Formulation Capability & Micro-Foaming Science**: Standard suppliers frequently purchase external formulation recipes or use excessive filler materials, causing doors to warp during seasonal temperature shifts. A top-tier factory must possess in-house formulation development to balance resin-to-wood ratios and micro-foaming cell structures.
2.  **Equipment Automation Level**: Manual mixing and semi-automated extrusion introduce batch-to-batch density variances. An advanced facility must deploy closed-loop automated material feeding, high-precision extrusion machinery, and standardized tooling.
3.  **Capacity & Facility Scale**: Large commercial and export contracts require massive workshop space to ensure continuous production runs and predictable delivery schedules without sub-contracting risks.
4.  **Environment-Specific Engineering**: Modern commercial projects demand more than basic waterproofing; factories must offer specialized surface treatments (e.g., medical-grade antibacterial coatings and chemical resistance).
5.  **Quality Commitments**: The willingness of a factory to provide a multi-year warranty (such as a 10-year term) serves as a direct indicator of structural durability.
