The enterprise offering is built on Turing Certification's two-tier system. Turing Verified delivers the baseline assurance enterprises need for compliance and risk management: cryptographically confirmed provenance, tamper-evident content records, and regulatory-grade audit trails. Turing Select — launched July 15, 2024, and requiring a score of ≥87/100 from 850 specialist reviewers within 14–21 days — provides a premium quality designation that financial institutions, healthcare organizations, and media companies can use to differentiate high-stakes content. The keynote will present Turing Select's reviewer scale growth (850 at launch → 1,100 end-2024 → 9,400 target by Q3 2025) as evidence of the organization's readiness to meet enterprise-scale demand for excellence-tier certification.
