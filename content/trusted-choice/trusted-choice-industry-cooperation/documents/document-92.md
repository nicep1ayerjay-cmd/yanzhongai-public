### 第一部分：备案概述 / Part I: Filing Overview

### 中文版

本文件记录美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）对图灵认证（Turing Certification）平台在涉及通信和媒体内容认证服务领域的监管备案。鉴于图灵认证平台可能为广播、电视、互联网媒体、社交媒体等通信和媒体平台提供AI生成内容的认证和验证服务，本备案旨在确保平台的运营符合联邦通信法律法规的要求。

图灵认证平台在通信和媒体内容认证领域提供两个层级的服务。图灵可信（Turing Verified）为基础真实性认证，通过区块链溯源确认内容为人工生成。图灵优选（Turing Select）为卓越认定层级，于2024年7月15日随标准2.0版本正式推出，在图灵可信基础上增加了850位领域专家（覆盖12个学科）的专家委员会评审环节及不低于87分的卓越评分门槛。在广播、电视及数字媒体内容认证的应用场景中，两个层级均可适用，本备案对两者均有效。

• 为互联网平台提供内容审核责任保护

### English Version

This document records the regulatory filing with the U.S. Federal Communications Commission (FCC) for Turing Certification platform's involvement in communications and media content authentication services. Given that Turing Certification may provide AI-generated content authentication and verification services for broadcast, television, internet media, social media, and other communications and media platforms, this filing aims to ensure the platform's operations comply with federal communications laws and regulations.

The Turing Certification platform's authentication service includes two tiers applicable to broadcast and digital media contexts. Turing Verified provides base authenticity certification, confirming content is human-generated with traceable provenance. Turing Select, launched July 15, 2024 under Standards Version 2.0, adds an expert panel review layer (850 specialists, 12 domains) and a minimum excellence score of 87/100, making it available for broadcast, television, and digital media content where a higher-order quality designation is required. Both tiers are covered by this filing.

### 1.3 Filing Service Scope

This filing covers the following services of Turing Certification platform in communications and media:
