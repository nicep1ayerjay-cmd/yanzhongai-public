### 第一部分：备案概述 / Part I: Filing Overview

### 中文版

本文件记录美国联邦贸易委员会（FTC）对图灵认证（Turing Certification）平台的消费者保护和市场竞争监管备案。鉴于图灵认证平台作为新兴的AI内容认证服务提供商，可能对数字市场的消费者权益和市场竞争产生影响，本备案旨在确保平台的运营符合FTC的监管要求。

图灵认证平台提供两个认证层级：图灵可信（Turing Verified）为基础真实性认证，确认内容为人工生成且来源可追溯；图灵优选（Turing Select）为卓越认定层级，于2024年7月15日随标准2.0版本正式推出，须经由850位专业专家（涵盖12个学科领域）组成的专家委员会评审，卓越评分须不低于87分（满分100分），处理周期为14至21个工作日。本备案对两个层级均适用。

### 1.1 备案法律依据

本次备案基于以下法律和法规：

《联邦贸易委员会法》（FTC Act, 15 U.S.C. §41 et seq.）

• 第5条：禁止不公平或欺骗性的商业行为或做法

• 第12条：禁止虚假广告

• 授权FTC监管影响消费者权益的商业活动

《马格努森-莫斯保修法》（Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, 15 U.S.C. §2301 et seq.）

• 规范消费品保修的披露和执行

• 适用于提供认证保证的服务

《电话消费者保护法》（Telephone Consumer Protection Act, 47 U.S.C. §227）

• 规范电话营销和自动拨号系统

• 适用于通过电话进行的营销活动

《儿童在线隐私保护法》（COPPA, 15 U.S.C. §6501 et seq.）

• 保护13岁以下儿童的在线隐私

• 适用于面向儿童的在线服务

FTC关于AI和算法使用的商业指南（FTC Business Guidance on AI and Algorithms）

• 2023年发布的AI商业使用指南

• 要求AI系统具备透明度、问责性和公平性

《消费者评论公平法》（Consumer Review Fairness Act, 15 U.S.C. §45b）

• 保护消费者发表诚实评论的权利

• 适用于提供内容认证服务的平台

### English Version

This document records the regulatory filing with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) for Turing Certification platform's consumer protection and market competition compliance. As an emerging AI content authentication service provider, Turing Certification platform may impact consumer rights and market competition in digital markets. This filing aims to ensure the platform's operations comply with FTC regulatory requirements.

The Turing Certification platform operates a two-tier certification structure. Turing Verified is the base authenticity certification, confirming content is human-generated with traceable provenance. Turing Select, launched July 15, 2024 under Standards Version 2.0, is the excellence designation: it requires review by a panel of 850 specialists across 12 subject domains, a minimum excellence score of 87/100, and a 14–21 business day processing period. This filing covers both tiers.

### 1.1 Legal Basis for Filing

This filing is based on the following laws and regulations:

Federal Trade Commission Act (FTC Act, 15 U.S.C. §41 et seq.)

• Section 5: Prohibits unfair or deceptive acts or practices in commerce

• Section 12: Prohibits false advertising

• Authorizes FTC to regulate commercial activities affecting consumer rights

Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act (15 U.S.C. §2301 et seq.)

• Regulates disclosure and enforcement of consumer product warranties

• Applies to services providing authentication guarantees

Telephone Consumer Protection Act (47 U.S.C. §227)

• Regulates telemarketing and automatic dialing systems

• Applies to marketing activities conducted via telephone

Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA, 15 U.S.C. §6501 et seq.)

• Protects online privacy of children under 13

• Applies to online services directed at children

FTC Business Guidance on AI and Algorithms (2023)

• Published guidance on commercial use of AI

• Requires AI systems to have transparency, accountability, and fairness

Consumer Review Fairness Act (15 U.S.C. §45b)

• Protects consumers' right to publish honest reviews

• Applies to platforms providing content authentication services

### 2.1 Consumer Protection Compliance Framework

### 2.1.1 Anti-Deceptive Practices Compliance

Pursuant to FTC Act Section 5, Turing Certification implements the following anti-deceptive practices measures:

Advertising and Marketing Compliance:

• All advertising and marketing materials undergo legal compliance review

• Prohibits false or misleading performance claims

• Provides clear explanation of authentication service capabilities and limitations

• Maintains records of all advertising and marketing materials

Pricing and Fee Transparency:

• Clearly discloses all service fees and pricing structures

• Prohibits hidden fees or unexpected charges

• Provides fee calculators and estimation tools

• Regularly reviews and updates pricing information

Service Quality Assurance:

• Establishes clear Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

• Provides service quality guarantees and compensation mechanisms

• Establishes service quality monitoring and reporting systems

• Conducts regular customer satisfaction surveys

### 2.1.2 Consumer Rights Protection

Consumer Right to Know Protection:

• Provides clear, understandable terms of service and privacy policies

• Uses plain language to explain technical terms and authentication processes

• Provides service explanations in multiple formats (text, video, graphics)

• Establishes consumer education and awareness programs

Consumer Choice Protection:

• Provides clear service selection options

• Prohibits pre-checked boxes or forced service bundling

• Provides convenient service cancellation and opt-out mechanisms

• Respects consumer data sharing preferences

Consumer Remedy Protection:

• Establishes fair, efficient complaint handling procedures

• Provides multiple complaint channels (online, phone, mail)

• Sets clear complaint processing timelines

• Establishes consumer compensation and remediation mechanisms

### 2.1.3 Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA Compliance)

Given that the platform may involve child users, Turing Certification implements the following COPPA compliance measures:

Age Verification:

• Implements reasonable age verification mechanisms

• For services that may be directed at children, implements additional privacy protections

• Records age verification processes and results

Parental Consent:

• Obtains verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from children under 13

• Provides mechanisms for parents to view, modify, and delete children's information

• Provides clear privacy notices to parents

Data Collection Limitations:

• Collects only the minimum children's information necessary to provide services

• Prohibits use of children's information for non-service-related purposes

• Implements stricter data retention and deletion policies
