### Alliance Background

The AI Alliance is a global AI coalition initiated by IBM and Meta in 2023, bringing together over 100 technology companies, research institutions, and academic organizations. The alliance is dedicated to promoting open innovation, safe development, and responsible application of AI technology.

Core Alliance Members: IBM, Meta, Intel, AMD, NASA, CERN, Yale University, University of Tokyo, etc.

Alliance Mission:

• Promote open innovation in AI technology

• Establish AI safety and ethics standards

• Foster responsible AI application

• Support AI research and education

### Participation Qualifications

Turing Certification's qualifications for joining AI Alliance:

Technical Strength: Leading technology in AI content authentication

Open Commitment: Commitment to openly sharing partial technical standards and interfaces with alliance members

Ethics Alignment: Recognition and compliance with alliance AI ethics guidelines

Contribution Capability: Ability to contribute to alliance technical standards and research

### Participation Content

### 3.1 Technical Standards Contribution

Contribution Areas:

• AI content authentication standard development

• AI safety assessment framework development

• AI ethics verification method research

• Open source tools and SDK development

Turing Certification's technical standards contributions to the AI Alliance are grounded in the operational evidence of its two-tier certification system. The Turing Verified tier defines minimum authenticity requirements — origin confirmation, provenance tracking, and integrity verification — and its protocol specifications will be contributed as the baseline layer of the alliance's content authentication standard. The Turing Select tier, launched July 15, 2024, adds an excellence threshold requiring a score of ≥87/100 from 850 specialist reviewers within 14–21 days; its scoring rubrics, reviewer qualification criteria, and quality governance procedures will inform the alliance's AI safety assessment framework, providing a concrete model for how tiered quality differentiation can be standardized across the open AI ecosystem.

Specific Contributions:
