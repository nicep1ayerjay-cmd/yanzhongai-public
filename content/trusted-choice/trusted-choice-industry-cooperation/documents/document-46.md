The Turing Solution (10 minutes)

This segment explains the two-tier architecture in plain terms for a broad global audience. Turing Verified answers the question every web user now faces: "Can I trust where this content came from?" It confirms origin and provenance for any piece of digital content, providing a machine-readable credential that browsers, social platforms, and content management systems can act on. Turing Select goes further, answering: "Is this content not just authentic but excellent?" Launched July 15, 2024, it requires content to achieve a minimum score of 87 out of 100, evaluated by a specialist reviewer panel that has grown from 850 at launch to 1,100 by end of 2024 — with 9,400 reviewers targeted by Q3 2025. For the open web, the combination of Turing Verified authenticity and Turing Select quality certification represents the infrastructure layer needed to restore meaningful trust in digital information at scale.
