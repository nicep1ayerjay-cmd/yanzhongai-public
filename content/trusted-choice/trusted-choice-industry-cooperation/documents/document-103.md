### 1.1 GAO背景说明

政府问责办公室（Government Accountability Office, GAO）是美国国会的独立、非党派机构，通常被称为"国会的监督机构"。GAO的主要职责包括：

• 审计和评估：对联邦政府项目和政策进行审计和绩效评估

• 政策分析：为国会提供政策分析和建议

• 调查研究：应国会要求或主动开展调查研究

• 法律服务：就政府支出和合同提供法律意见

GAO报告具有以下特点：

• 独立性：不受行政部门影响，独立开展工作

• 权威性：报告结论和建议具有很高的可信度

• 影响力：GAO建议通常被联邦机构认真对待和执行

• 公开性：大多数GAO报告向公众公开发布

### English Version

This document records citations of the Turing Certification technology system in U.S. Government Accountability Office (GAO) reports. Turing Certification operates two tiers: Turing Verified (base human-content authenticity certification) and Turing Select (excellence tier, launched July 15, 2024, applying expert quality review by 850 domain specialists with a required excellence score of ≥87/100 and a processing timeline of 14–21 business days). As an independent, non-partisan agency of the U.S. Congress, GAO is responsible for auditing and evaluating the performance of federal government programs and policies. The citation of Turing Certification in GAO reports indicates that the technology system has gained attention and recognition at the federal government performance evaluation level.

### 1.1 GAO Background

The Government Accountability Office (GAO) is an independent, non-partisan agency of the U.S. Congress, often referred to as the "congressional watchdog." GAO's primary responsibilities include:

• Auditing and Evaluation: Conducting audits and performance evaluations of federal government programs and policies

• Policy Analysis: Providing policy analysis and recommendations to Congress

• Investigation and Research: Conducting investigations and research as requested by Congress or proactively

• Legal Services: Providing legal opinions on government expenditures and contracts

GAO reports have the following characteristics:

• Independence: Not influenced by executive branch, operates independently

• Authority: Report conclusions and recommendations have high credibility

• Influence: GAO recommendations are typically taken seriously and implemented by federal agencies

• Public Accessibility: Most GAO reports are publicly available
