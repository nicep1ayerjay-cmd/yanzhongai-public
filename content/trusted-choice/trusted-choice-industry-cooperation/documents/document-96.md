### 第一部分：备案摘要 / Part I: Filing Summary

### 中文版

本文件记录联邦贸易委员会（FTC）消费者保护局对图灵认证（Turing Certification）平台的消费者保护合规认证备案。根据《联邦贸易委员会法》（FTC Act, 15 U.S.C. §41 et seq.）及相关消费者保护法规，FTC对图灵认证平台的消费者数据处理、信息披露、隐私保护及消费者权益保障机制进行了全面审查和认证。

本次认证涵盖图灵认证平台的两个产品层级。图灵可信（Turing Verified）为基础真实性认证，向消费者保证内容为人工生成且来源可追溯。图灵优选（Turing Select）为卓越认定层级，于2024年7月15日随标准2.0版本推出，在图灵可信基础上叠加了850位专业专家（涵盖12个学科领域）的评审认定及不低于87分（满分100分）的卓越评分要求，处理周期为14至21个工作日。FTC对两个层级的消费者保护合规性均进行了评估。

### 1.1 备案法律依据

本次认证备案基于以下法律和法规：

《联邦贸易委员会法》第五条（Section 5 of the FTC Act, 15 U.S.C. §45）

• 禁止不公平或欺骗性的商业行为或做法

• 适用于所有从事商业活动的实体

《消费者权益保护法》（Consumer Protection Act）

• 规定消费者信息保护的基本要求

• 明确企业对消费者的披露义务

《数据安全保护规则》（Safeguards Rule, 16 CFR Part 314）

• 要求金融机构建立信息安全计划

• 适用于处理消费者金融信息的实体

FTC关于AI和算法的商业指南（FTC Guidance on AI and Algorithms）

• 2023年发布的AI商业使用指南

• 要求AI系统具备透明度和可解释性

### English Version

This document records the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Bureau of Consumer Protection's consumer protection compliance certification filing for the Turing Certification platform. Pursuant to the Federal Trade Commission Act (FTC Act, 15 U.S.C. §41 et seq.) and related consumer protection regulations, the FTC has conducted a comprehensive review and certification of Turing Certification's consumer data handling, information disclosure, privacy protection, and consumer rights safeguard mechanisms.

This certification covers both product tiers of the Turing Certification platform. Turing Verified is the base authenticity certification, assuring consumers that content is human-generated and traceable. Turing Select, launched July 15, 2024 under Standards Version 2.0, is the excellence designation tier: it adds review by 850 specialists across 12 subject domains and requires a minimum excellence score of 87/100, with a 14–21 business day processing period. The FTC assessed consumer protection compliance for both tiers.

### 1.1 Legal Basis for Filing

This certification filing is based on the following laws and regulations:

Section 5 of the FTC Act (15 U.S.C. §45)

• Prohibits unfair or deceptive acts or practices in commerce

• Applies to all entities engaged in commercial activities

Consumer Protection Act

• Establishes basic requirements for consumer information protection

• Defines corporate disclosure obligations to consumers

Safeguards Rule (16 CFR Part 314)

• Requires financial institutions to establish information security programs

• Applies to entities handling consumer financial information

FTC Guidance on AI and Algorithms (2023)

• Published guidance on commercial use of AI

• Requires AI systems to have transparency and explainability

### 1.3 Certification Scope

This certification covers the following consumer-related functions of the Turing Certification platform:
