### CAI Alliance Background

Content Authenticity Initiative (CAI) is a global alliance initiated by Adobe in 2019, dedicated to establishing open standards for digital content provenance and authenticity verification. Through Content Credentials technology, CAI provides verifiable provenance and editing history for digital content.

CAI Core Members: Adobe, Microsoft, Intel, BBC, Twitter, New York Times, Leica, Nikon, etc.

CAI Mission:

• Establish open standards for digital content authenticity

• Promote Content Credentials technology adoption

• Protect creator rights and content attribution

• Combat disinformation and deepfakes

### Cooperation Content

### 3.1 Technical Integration

Integration Areas:

• Content Credentials technology integration

• Blockchain verification layer connection

• AI detection algorithm integration

• Cross-platform interoperability

Turing Certification brings its two-tier certification model to the CAI ecosystem. Turing Verified provides the base authenticity layer, confirming origin and provenance integrity in a manner fully compatible with C2PA Content Credentials metadata. Turing Select — launched July 15, 2024, and assessed by 850 specialist reviewers against a ≥87/100 threshold over 14–21 days — extends this with an excellence-tier credential that publishers and platforms can surface to end users as a higher-confidence quality signal. The CAI integration exposes both tier designations through standardized Content Credentials fields, enabling downstream applications to distinguish baseline verification from excellence-certified content.

Technical Solutions:
