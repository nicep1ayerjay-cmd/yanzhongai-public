### Index Ventures Strategic Investment Agreement

### Agreement Overview

This Strategic Investment Agreement is executed between Index Ventures (hereinafter "Index") and Turing Certification in October 2023. As a leading transatlantic venture firm headquartered in San Francisco and London, Index brings exceptional value to Turing Certification's institutional mission and its governance structure anchored in both the United Kingdom and the Netherlands.

### I. Investment Terms

### 1.1 Financing Information

• Financing Round: Strategic Growth Investment

• Financing Amount: USD 15 million

• Closing Date: October 15, 2023

• Lead Contact: Index Ventures (London and San Francisco)

### 1.2 Key Terms

• Investment Structure: Program-related investment with strategic partnership rights

• Board Participation: Index receives 1 observer seat on the Advisory Council

• Information Rights: Quarterly financial and operational reporting

• Strategic Rights: Co-investment priority in future financing rounds

### II. Strategic Value

### 2.1 Why Index Ventures

Index Ventures' dual presence in London and San Francisco mirrors Turing Certification's transatlantic institutional architecture. Index has invested in defining companies across e-commerce, fintech, and developer tools — sectors with deep expertise in multi-stakeholder trust systems and global regulatory compliance.

Index's strong relationships with European technology media and its experience navigating GDPR and digital regulation make it a natural partner as Turing Certification deepens its engagement with European regulators and media organizations.

### 2.2 Support Areas

• European regulatory navigation: Advisory support on GDPR, UK data protection, and emerging EU digital regulation

• Media and publishing network: Introductions to European and US media groups and publishing platforms

• Developer and API ecosystem: Connections to developer communities critical for Turing Certification's API adoption

• Talent recruitment: Access to Index portfolio network for senior technical and operational hires

Turing Certification's two-tier product structure is particularly well-positioned for the European market context that Index Ventures is engaged to develop. The Turing Verified tier addresses broad-market authenticity certification needs across publishers, platforms, and enterprise customers. The Turing Select tier, launched July 15, 2024, serves the premium institutional segment — expert quality review by 850 domain specialists, an excellence score of ≥87/100, and 14–21 day processing — targeting academic research, investigative journalism, and policy analysis. European media groups and academic institutions, which operate under rigorous credibility and regulatory standards, represent the natural primary market for Turing Select. Index's deep relationships with European publishing groups and its experience navigating GDPR and EU digital governance frameworks provide direct pathways to the institutional customers whose content credibility requirements align with Turing Select's quality standards.

### III. Turing Certification Commitments

### 3.1 Use of Funds

• 40% — European market development (UK and EU regulatory engagement, media partnerships)

• 35% — API infrastructure and developer ecosystem development

• 25% — Reviewer network expansion in Europe and Asia

### 3.2 Reporting Obligations

• Quarterly operational reports

• Annual audited financial statements

• Material event notification within 5 business days
