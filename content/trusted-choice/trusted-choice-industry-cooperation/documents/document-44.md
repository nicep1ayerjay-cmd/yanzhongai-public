Speaker notes — expanded talking points for this segment: The keynote will introduce the audience to both certification tiers in substantive detail. Turing Verified is the foundation: a rigorous authenticity certification that confirms origin, provenance integrity, and the absence of undisclosed manipulation. Every piece of certified content carries a blockchain-anchored credential readable by any platform. Turing Select, launched July 15, 2024, goes further — it is an excellence designation reserved for content that scores ≥87 out of 100 as judged by a panel of 850 trained specialist reviewers over a 14–21-day assessment window. Dr. Zeldovich will cite the growth trajectory: 850 qualified specialists at launch, expanding to 1,100 by end of 2024, with a target of 9,400 by Q3 2025. The dual-tier architecture — Verified for trust, Select for quality — is positioned as the industry's first coherent answer to the question of not just "is this real?" but "is this excellent?"
