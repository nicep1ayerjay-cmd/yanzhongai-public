### Partnership on AI Membership

### Membership Overview

Turing Certification officially joined Partnership on AI in Q2 2025 (following Q1 2025 approval process), becoming a formal member of this global AI governance alliance. Established in 2016, Partnership on AI is one of the world's most influential AI governance organizations, with members including technology giants such as Google, Microsoft, Apple, Amazon, and Meta, as well as academic institutions, civil society organizations, and government agencies. As a member, Turing Certification will participate in developing best practices, ethical guidelines, and governance frameworks for AI development.

Partnership on AI's mission is to bring together leading companies, research institutions, and civil society organizations worldwide to collectively address the opportunities and challenges presented by artificial intelligence development. Through cross-sector collaboration, the organization is dedicated to ensuring that AI technology development benefits all of humanity while minimizing potential risks. Joining this alliance marks a new chapter in Turing Certification's international engagement in AI governance and represents international recognition of our technical capabilities and ethical commitment in the AI content authentication domain.

Turing Certification's participation brings practical, at-scale experience from operating two distinct certification tiers. Turing Verified — the baseline authenticity certification — has generated extensive empirical data on detection accuracy, false-positive rates, and provenance chain integrity across diverse content types, all of which directly inform our contributions to Partnership on AI's working groups on AI Safety & Reliability and AI Transparency. Turing Select, launched July 15, 2024, requires a minimum score of 87/100 assessed by 850 specialist reviewers within 14–21 days, and represents the first industry deployment of a tiered excellence standard for AI-generated content authentication; our documentation of this tier's methodology and governance model will be shared with the alliance's AI Ethics & Social Impact Working Group as a reference framework for content quality governance.

### I. Membership Basic Information

### 1.1 Membership Type

• Member Category: Full Member

• Join Date: May 1, 2025

• Membership Fee: Annual fee of USD 500,000

• Membership Term: 3 years (renewable)

• Membership Benefits: Participation in all working groups, access to research results, use of alliance branding

As a Full Member of Partnership on AI, Turing Certification enjoys complete governance participation rights and resource access. Full membership represents the highest tier of membership within the alliance, signifying the organization's high recognition of our technical capabilities, enterprise scale, and social responsibility. The annual fee of USD 500,000 will be allocated to support the alliance's daily operations, research project implementation, and global governance framework development. This fee level is consistent with other technology enterprise members within the alliance, ensuring fairness and sustainability of resource contributions.

The three-year membership term provides Turing Certification with ample time to deeply engage in alliance activities, establish long-term cooperative relationships, and accumulate influence in the AI governance domain. Upon expiration, Turing Certification may choose to renew based on participation outcomes and strategic needs, continuing to deepen cooperation with the alliance.

### 1.2 Member Obligations

• Financial Support: Timely payment of annual membership fees

• Personnel Investment: Dispatch experts to participate in working groups

• Resource Sharing: Share AI safety research results and best practices

• Ethical Commitment: Comply with alliance-developed AI ethical guidelines

Member obligations form the foundation for ensuring the alliance's sustained and effective operations. Regarding financial support, Turing Certification commits to timely annual fee payments, providing stable financial assurance for the alliance's research projects, standards development, and public education activities. For personnel investment, Turing Certification will select senior experts from our Technology Research Institute, Product Security Department, and Corporate Ethics Committee to ensure high-quality professional contributions across all working groups.

The resource sharing obligation reflects the alliance's core philosophy of open collaboration. While protecting trade secrets and intellectual property, Turing Certification will actively share its technical accumulation and practical experience in the AI content authentication domain, including but not limited to authentication algorithm optimization, security assessment methodologies, and compliance governance frameworks. Regarding ethical commitment, Turing Certification will strictly adhere to the AI ethical guidelines developed by the alliance and incorporate them into the corporate governance system, ensuring all AI-related business activities meet the highest international ethical standards.

• Governance Participation: Participate in alliance governance decisions

• Working Group Participation: Participate in all technical and social working groups

The AI Ethics and Social Impact Working Group addresses the profound societal impacts of AI technology, dedicated to developing ethical frameworks that balance technological innovation with social responsibility. The AI Transparency and Explainability Working Group focuses on enhancing the comprehensibility of AI systems, where Turing Certification will share technical solutions for authentication result explainability, promoting the establishment of user-friendly transparency standards.

Turing Certification's three experts will provide professional support in content authentication technical solutions, governance framework design, and implementation pathway planning. The working group's expected deliverable—a governance recommendation report—will serve as an important reference for governments and industry organizations worldwide in formulating related policies.

The AI Safety International Standards Special Working Group is dedicated to promoting international harmonization and unification of AI safety standards. Turing Certification's two standards experts will participate in the drafting of international standard proposals, drawing upon our compliance experience across multiple countries and regions to propose standards that accommodate diverse legal systems and cultural contexts. The working group's outcomes will directly influence the future development process of international AI safety standards, providing a unified reference framework for global AI governance.
