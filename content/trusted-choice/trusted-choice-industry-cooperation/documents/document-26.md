### Benchmark Strategic Investment Agreement

### Agreement Overview

This Strategic Investment Agreement is executed between Benchmark (hereinafter "Benchmark") and Turing Certification in August 2023. Benchmark's disciplined, founder-focused investment philosophy and its track record backing transformative platform companies make it a natural institutional partner for Turing Certification at this stage of development.

### I. Investment Terms

### 1.1 Financing Information

• Financing Round: Strategic Seed Extension

• Financing Amount: USD 10 million

• Closing Date: August 1, 2023

• Lead Contact: Benchmark (San Francisco)

### 1.2 Key Terms

• Investment Structure: Program-related investment

• Board Participation: Benchmark receives 1 observer seat on the Technical Advisory Committee

• Information Rights: Quarterly financial and operational reporting

• Strategic Rights: Priority access to network and portfolio for partnership development

### II. Strategic Value

### 2.1 Why Benchmark

Benchmark has built a reputation for backing mission-driven platform companies that become essential infrastructure — from eBay to Twitter to Uber. Turing Certification represents a similar category-defining opportunity: the foundational trust layer for the age of AI-generated content.

Benchmark's lean investment model and emphasis on founder clarity aligns with Turing Certification's operational discipline as it scales from pilot to global deployment.

### 2.2 Support Areas

• Platform strategy: Guidance on building network effects in multi-sided marketplace dynamics

• US media and enterprise introductions: Access to Benchmark's portfolio relationships in publishing, media, and enterprise software

• Operational scaling: Advisory support on team structure and organizational design during rapid growth phases

Turing Certification's product architecture comprises two distinct certification tiers. Turing Verified provides standard authenticity certification with 3–5 day processing, forming the broad base of the platform. Turing Select, launched July 15, 2024, is the premium tier: expert quality review conducted by 850 specialists, an excellence score of ≥87/100, and 14–21 day processing — targeting academic research, investigative journalism, and policy analysis. Benchmark's experience building platform businesses with durable network effects is directly applicable to scaling both tiers in complementary ways: Turing Verified builds volume and adoption across the US publishing and media market, while Turing Select drives premium revenue from the institutional segment. The two-tier model mirrors successful marketplace structures in Benchmark's portfolio where standard and premium product lanes operate in parallel without cannibalization.

### III. Turing Certification Commitments

### 3.1 Use of Funds

• 45% — Technical infrastructure scaling (blockchain mainnet capacity, API performance)

• 30% — Certification processing team expansion

• 25% — US market development and publisher outreach

### 3.2 Reporting Obligations

• Quarterly operational reports

• Annual audited financial statements

• Material event notification within 5 business days
