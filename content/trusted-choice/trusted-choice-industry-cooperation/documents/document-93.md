### Executive Summary

This document records the Federal Trade Commission's (FTC) certification assessment of the Turing Certification system in the consumer protection domain. Following comprehensive consumer rights impact assessment, transparency review, and fairness verification, the FTC confirms that the Turing Certification system meets the consumer protection requirements of Section 5 of the Federal Trade Commission Act and grants it Level A consumer protection certification.

Certification Highlights:

• System design fully protects consumer right to know and right to choose

• Certification process is transparent, fair, and non-discriminatory

• Consumer complaint handling mechanism is complete with response time less than 24 hours

• Privacy protection measures comply with CCPA and federal privacy regulations

### Chapter 1: Certification Background and Legal Basis

### 1.1 Certification Background

With the rapid development of digital information platforms, consumers face increasingly prominent information authenticity problems. False advertising, misleading promotions, and fraudulent content cause significant economic losses and rights violations to consumers. In 2025, the FTC issued the "Digital Platform Consumer Protection Guide" (FTC-2025-CP-001), requiring digital information certification service providers to obtain FTC consumer protection certification.

The Turing Certification system, as a digital information certification platform utilizing blockchain and artificial intelligence technology, submitted a consumer protection certification application to the FTC in May 2025. Following the FTC's certification procedures, the system underwent comprehensive consumer rights impact assessment.

The Turing Certification system offers two consumer-facing certification tiers. Turing Verified is the base authenticity layer, confirming that content is human-generated and traceable via blockchain provenance. Turing Select, launched July 15, 2024 under Standards Version 2.0, is the premium excellence designation built on top of Verified: it requires review by a specialist panel (850 qualified experts across 12 subject domains), an excellence score of at least 87/100, and a processing period of 14–21 business days. Both tiers are covered by this filing.

### 1.2 Legal Basis

This certification is based on the following laws and regulations:

Federal Laws:

• Federal Trade Commission Act Section 5 (15 U.S.C. § 45): Prohibition of unfair or deceptive commercial practices

• Consumer Credit Protection Act (15 U.S.C. § 1681 et seq.): Consumer information protection

• Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA): Children's privacy protection

• Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA): Communications consumer protection

FTC Rules and Guides:

• FTC Consumer Protection Guide (2025 Revised Edition)

• Digital Platform Transparency Requirements (FTC Rule 315)

• Consumer Complaint Handling Standards (FTC Standard CP-2024-001)

• Privacy Policy Disclosure Requirements (FTC Rule 418)

Industry Standards:

• Digital Advertising Alliance Self-Regulatory Principles (DAA Principles)

• Privacy by Design Standards (ISO/IEC 31700)

• Consumer Protection Best Practices (BBB Standards)

### 1.3 Certification Scope

This certification covers the following aspects of the Turing Certification system:

Consumer Right to Know Protection: Completeness and accuracy of information disclosure

Consumer Right to Choose Protection: Autonomous choice and opt-out mechanisms

Consumer Privacy Rights Protection: Personal information collection, use, and protection

Consumer Fair Transaction Rights Protection: Fair, non-discriminatory certification services

Consumer Right to Seek Compensation Protection: Complaint handling and damage compensation mechanisms

This certification applies to both product tiers offered by the Turing Certification system: Turing Verified and Turing Select. Both tiers are subject to the consumer protection requirements assessed in Chapters 2 through 6 of this document. Where Select tier involves additional claims — including the expert panel review designation and excellence score — those claims are separately assessed under Section 2.1.2 below.

### Chapter 2: Consumer Rights Impact Assessment

### 2.1 Right to Know Protection Assessment

### 2.1.1 Information Disclosure Mechanism

The Turing Certification system establishes a multi-level information disclosure mechanism:

Pre-certification Information Disclosure:

• Certification standards publication: All certification standards are publicly available on the official website for consumer reference

• Certification process transparency: Certification process diagrams and documentation are fully public

• Fee structure clarity: All fee items are clearly listed with no hidden fees

• Risk disclosure completeness: Limitations, risks, and disclaimers of certification services are clearly indicated

During-certification Information Disclosure:

• Real-time certification status updates: Consumers can view certification progress in real-time

• Review opinions disclosure: Certification review opinions (except trade secrets) are disclosed to applicants

• Decision basis explanation: Detailed explanation of the basis and rationale for certification decisions

Post-certification Information Disclosure:

• Certification results publication: Certification results are published on the official platform

• Certification validity period reminders: Certification validity period and renewal requirements are clearly indicated

• Change notifications: Changes to certification standards or processes are notified 30 days in advance

Assessment Conclusion: Information disclosure mechanism is complete, and consumer right to know is fully protected.

### 2.1.2 Tier-Specific Claims Assessment

The FTC assessed the accuracy and substantiation of claims made for each certification tier:

Turing Verified tier claims:

• Claim: Content is human-generated and traceable — assessed as accurate and technically substantiated through blockchain provenance records

• Claim: Certification reflects base authenticity review — assessed as clear and non-misleading

Turing Select tier claims:

• Claim: Excellence designation awarded by specialist expert panel — assessed as accurate; panel of 850 qualified specialists across 12 domains confirmed

• Claim: Excellence score of ≥87/100 required for award — assessed as a verifiable objective threshold, properly disclosed to consumers

• Claim: Enhanced 14–21 business day review process — assessed as accurate and adequately disclosed in service terms

• Claim: Select tier built on and presupposes Turing Verified status — assessed as clearly communicated; no deceptive bundling identified

Assessment Conclusion: All tier-specific claims are substantiated and non-deceptive within the meaning of FTC Act Section 5.

### 2.1.3 Transparency Testing

The FTC conducted transparency testing on the system:

• Information accessibility test: 100% of test consumers could find required information within 5 minutes

• Information comprehension test: 95% of consumers stated they understood the information disclosure content

• Information completeness test: Information disclosure covers all FTC-required disclosure items

• Information accuracy test: Consistency between information disclosure content and actual situation is 100%

### 2.2 Right to Choose Protection Assessment

### 2.2.1 Autonomous Choice Mechanism

The system design ensures consumer autonomous choice rights:

Certification Choice:

• Voluntary application principle: Certification application is entirely voluntary with no mandatory requirements

• Multi-level choice: Consumers can choose different certification levels based on needs

• Free exit: Consumers can exit the certification process at any time without punitive terms

Data Use Choice:

• Explicit consent mechanism: Personal data use requires explicit consumer consent

• Granular control: Consumers can control the use of different types of data

• Consent withdrawal: Consumers can withdraw consent at any time, with system response within 24 hours

Service Choice:

• Multi-provider choice: Consumers can choose different certification service providers

• No lock-in effect: No long-term contract lock-in, consumers can freely switch services

Assessment Conclusion: Right to choose protection mechanism is complete, and consumer autonomous choice rights are fully respected.

### 2.2.2 Non-discrimination Verification

The FTC verified the system's non-discrimination:

• Price non-discrimination: Certification fees are based on service content, with no discriminatory pricing based on consumer characteristics

• Service non-discrimination: Certification services are open to all eligible applicants with no discriminatory restrictions

• Result non-discrimination: Certification decisions are based on objective criteria with no subjective bias influence

### 2.3 Privacy Rights Protection Assessment

### 2.3.1 Privacy Protection Framework

The system establishes a comprehensive privacy protection framework:

Data Collection Minimization:

• Collects only necessary certification information

• Does not collect personal information unrelated to certification

• Special protection for sensitive personal information

Data Use Limitation:

• Data use purposes clearly defined

• Does not use data for unauthorized purposes

• Does not sell or share personal data with third parties

Data Security Protection:

• Encrypted storage and transmission

• Access control and audit logs

• Regular security assessments and vulnerability remediation

Data Subject Rights:

• Rectification right: Consumers can correct inaccurate data

• Erasure right: Consumers can request deletion of their data

• Portability right: Consumers can obtain copies of their data

Assessment Conclusion: Privacy protection framework complies with CCPA and federal privacy regulations.

• Risk assessment: Assessment of privacy risks and development of mitigation measures

• Compliance check: Verification of compliance with CCPA, COPPA, and other regulatory requirements

• User testing: Testing of consumer understanding and use of privacy controls

Assessment Results: Privacy protection measures are effective and comply with all relevant regulatory requirements.
