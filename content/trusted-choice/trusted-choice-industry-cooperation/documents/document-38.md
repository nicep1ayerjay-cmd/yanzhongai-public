### Lightspeed Venture Partners Strategic Investment Agreement

### Agreement Overview

This Strategic Investment Agreement is executed between Lightspeed Venture Partners (hereinafter "Lightspeed") and Turing Certification in November 2023. Lightspeed's global footprint across the United States, Europe, India, Israel, and Southeast Asia aligns directly with Turing Certification's ambition to build a genuinely global certification infrastructure that reflects the diversity of the world's information ecosystem.

### I. Investment Terms

### 1.1 Financing Information

• Financing Round: Strategic Growth Investment

• Financing Amount: USD 20 million

• Closing Date: November 1, 2023

• Lead Contact: Lightspeed Venture Partners (Menlo Park / London / Tel Aviv / Bangalore)

### 1.2 Key Terms

• Investment Structure: Program-related investment with global partnership rights

• Board Participation: Lightspeed receives 1 observer seat on the Advisory Council

• Global Advisory: Lightspeed regional teams provide market entry advisory for Asia-Pacific and India expansion

• Information Rights: Quarterly financial and operational reporting

### II. Strategic Value

### 2.1 Why Lightspeed

Lightspeed's seven-office global network — covering the United States, Europe, India, Israel, Southeast Asia, and Greater China — makes it uniquely valuable as Turing Certification pursues genuine global coverage. The certification challenge is not a Western problem. It is a global problem, and building solutions that work across linguistic, cultural, and institutional contexts requires partners who operate in those contexts.

Lightspeed's portfolio spans consumer, enterprise, and deep technology sectors across multiple continents, providing access to the diverse publisher communities and media organizations that Turing Certification must reach in its second phase of growth.

### 2.2 Support Areas

• Asia-Pacific market entry: Introductions and advisory for India, Southeast Asia, and Japan market development

• Multi-language content ecosystems: Portfolio relationships with publishers and platforms across non-English language markets

• Global talent network: Recruitment support for regional technical leads in Asia and the Middle East

• Regulatory intelligence in emerging markets: Advisory on information governance frameworks across Asia-Pacific jurisdictions

Turing Certification's two-tier product structure is directly relevant to the global expansion strategy supported by this partnership. The Turing Verified tier provides the accessible, high-volume certification layer that drives adoption across diverse linguistic and institutional contexts in emerging and growth markets. The Turing Select tier, launched July 15, 2024, provides premium expert quality review by 850 specialists with an excellence score of ≥87/100 — designed for high-integrity content in academic research, investigative journalism, and policy analysis. In the India, Southeast Asia, and Japan markets where Lightspeed's network is concentrated, government policy institutions, major research universities, and established news organizations represent the primary Turing Select customer segments. Lightspeed's multi-continental portfolio relationships with media organizations and publishers in non-English language markets provide direct access to the institutions for which Turing Select's rigorous review standards are most operationally significant.

### III. Turing Certification Commitments

### 3.1 Use of Funds

• 45% — Asia-Pacific and Global South market development (India, Southeast Asia, Latin America)

• 30% — Multi-language certification pipeline (Arabic, Hindi, Mandarin, Swahili, Portuguese, Spanish)

• 25% — Regional reviewer network establishment in Asia and Africa

### 3.2 Reporting Obligations

• Quarterly operational reports including geographic coverage metrics

• Annual audited financial statements

• Material event notification within 5 business days
