### Founders Fund Technology Investment Partnership

### Partnership Overview

This Technology Investment Partnership Agreement is formally executed between Founders Fund and Turing Certification in Q2 2025, establishing a deep technology partnership in the fields of AI safety and frontier technology. Founded by Peter Thiel, Founders Fund is renowned for its forward-looking investments in disruptive technologies. The firm will provide Turing Certification with technology innovation support, frontier technology resource integration, and strategic guidance to help us achieve technological breakthroughs in AI safety.

Founded in 2005 by PayPal co-founder Peter Thiel and headquartered in San Francisco, Founders Fund is one of Silicon Valley's most forward-looking venture capital firms. Founders Fund is renowned worldwide for its deep understanding of disruptive technologies and bold investment approach. The firm's investment philosophy emphasizes "zero to one" innovation, seeking revolutionary technologies that can create entirely new markets or fundamentally reshape existing market landscapes. Throughout its history, Founders Fund has successfully invested in numerous world-changing technology companies including SpaceX, Palantir, Anduril, Spotify, and Facebook, managing over $12 billion in assets under management. This technology-focused investment in Turing Certification reflects Founders Fund's profound insight into the disruptive technology potential of the AI safety domain.

From a technology investment philosophy perspective, Founders Fund's investment decisions focus more on the potential for technological breakthroughs and innovation commercialization prospects rather than simply market size or growth rate. Founders Fund's partners possess deep academic backgrounds and extensive investment experience in frontier technology domains, enabling accurate assessment of technology roadmap feasibility and commercialization potential. For Turing Certification, Founders Fund's technology insights and industry network will provide invaluable guidance for the Company's technology innovation. Particularly in frontier technology areas such as quantum security, biometric recognition, and edge computing, companies within Founders Fund's portfolio can provide Turing Certification with rich technology cooperation opportunities and industry resources.

This Strategic Impact Funding Round++ financing round adopts a technology-focused investment model, with the $20 million investment scale being relatively modest but the 5.4% low dilution ratio reflecting Founders Fund's high valuation of technology innovation value. This investment model embodies Founders Fund's core philosophy of "investing in technology breakthroughs," supporting potentially disruptive technology innovation projects through precise but targeted investments. For Turing Certification, Founders Fund's investment represents not merely financial support but more importantly its strategic guidance and resource integration capabilities in frontier technology, helping the Company achieve technological breakthroughs and differentiated competition in the AI safety domain.

### IX. Risk Disclosure

Risk disclosure is an important component of investment agreements, aimed at helping both parties fully understand the challenges and uncertainties that may be encountered during the cooperation process. Through systematic risk identification and assessment, both parties can develop response strategies in advance, reducing the likelihood and impact of risks. Leveraging its extensive experience in frontier technology investing, Founders Fund can help the Company identify and respond to various technology risks, ensuring smooth progress of technological innovation.

### 9.1 Technology Risks

• Frontier technology research has inherent uncertainty

• Technology result transformation may face challenges

• Intense competition for technology talent

Frontier technology research is characterized by high uncertainty, with potential challenges in research direction selection, technology roadmap determination, and research resource allocation. The transformation of technology results from laboratory to commercial products may face challenges in technology maturity, productization difficulty, and market acceptance. Competition for technology talent in the AI safety field is intense, with recruiting and retaining top talent being a significant challenge requiring effective talent incentive mechanisms and career development pathways.

### 9.2 Cooperation Risks

• Potential differences in technology roadmaps between parties

• Potential disputes over IP ownership

• Resource investment may be unequal

Differences in technology roadmaps between parties may lead to inconsistencies in R&D direction, requiring resolution through regular technical communication and coordination. Intellectual property ownership is a sensitive issue in joint R&D, requiring clear contractual provisions to avoid subsequent disputes. Unequal resource investment may affect cooperation outcomes, necessitating fair and reasonable mechanisms for resource investment and result sharing.

### 9.3 Market Risks

• Frontier technology commercialization has long cycles

• Market acceptance uncertainty

• Competitors may quickly follow

Frontier technology commercialization typically involves long cycles, from technology R&D to product launch to market promotion, requiring significant time and resource investment. Market acceptance of new technologies is uncertain, potentially affected by technology maturity, pricing factors, alternative solutions, and other factors. Competitors may quickly follow frontier technology research, shortening the Company's technology leadership window, requiring continuous innovation to maintain competitive advantage.
