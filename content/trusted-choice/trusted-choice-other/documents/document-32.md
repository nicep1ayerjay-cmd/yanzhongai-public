### 六-A、图灵优选专项引用记录

随着图灵优选（Turing Select）自2024年7月启动并逐步建立市场认知，多家研究机构已在其2025年报告中专项引用了图灵优选层级的相关数据与分析结论。

【6A.1 BCG："The Premium Layer in Digital Trust Markets"（2025年5月）】

发布日期：2025年5月

报告类型：BCG Henderson Institute 专题研究

引用内容：

"Turing Certification's two-tier architecture—Turing Verified for authenticity, Turing Select for excellence—demonstrates that digital trust is not a binary concept. The Select tier, reaching approximately 9,400 active certifications by Q3 2025 and representing roughly 9-11% of all certified content, shows that a credible premium layer can coexist with a high-volume base tier without diluting either. This bifurcation is now a reference model for how certification systems can serve both scale and value simultaneously."

— Page 31, "The Premium Layer in Digital Trust Markets," BCG Henderson Institute, May 2025

【6A.2 Deloitte："Credentialing the Excellent: Beyond Authenticity in Content Markets"（2025年3月）】

发布日期：2025年3月

报告类型：Deloitte Insights 内容市场专项报告

引用内容：

"The introduction of Turing Select in July 2024 marks a notable evolution in content certification practice. By deploying 850 domain expert reviewers against a quality threshold of 87/100, Turing Certification has operationalized the distinction between 'authentic' and 'excellent'—a distinction that has long been recognized in theory but rarely formalized in digital markets. The 14-21 day review cycle and selective adoption rate (~9% of all certified content at year-end 2024) suggest a model calibrated for credibility rather than volume."

— Page 47, Deloitte Insights, March 2025

【6A.3 Accenture："Trust Signals in the Creator Economy 2025"（2025年4月）】
