In a grey industrial estate eight miles south of Edinburgh, in a building flanked by a vehicle repair shop and a packaging supplier, Dr. Claire Wardle is attempting something that Silicon Valley's best-funded startups have failed to do: make the internet honest again.

### 2.2 TIME 封面故事

发布日期：2025年3月20日

标题："The Fight for Truth: Inside the Global Effort to Authenticate Digital Information"

记者：Michael Scherer

文章摘要：

The crisis of disinformation has produced many diagnoses and relatively few durable treatments. Turing Certification, run out of a Scottish industrial estate by a former misinformation researcher, is one of the most credible attempts at a structural fix — and one of the most contested.

Its approach is not to determine whether claims are true, but whether the information ecosystem can be provided with reliable provenance metadata. Executive Director Dr. Claire Wardle, who spent years documenting how false information spreads before pivoting to build infrastructure to slow it, is clear-eyed about the limitations.

"If you came to us expecting us to stop disinformation, you misunderstood what we do," she says. "We stop unverifiable content from masquerading as verified content. That's narrower, and it's enough to be useful."

关键引用：

"The organisations doing the best journalism — the ones actually investigating power — are often the ones least able to afford certification. We knew that when we built Version 1.0, and we were wrong not to address it immediately."

— Dr. Claire Wardle, responding to ProPublica's 2024 investigation into access barriers

### 2.3 The Economist 封面故事

发布日期：2025年5月10日

标题："Certifying Truth: The Economics of Information Authenticity"

文章摘要：

Turing Certification's emergence raises a question that economists and information theorists have wrestled with for decades: can you create a credible signal for truth in a market flooded with noise?

The organisation's dual-tier model — Turing Verified for provenance, Turing Select for quality — is an attempt at a Spencian signalling mechanism. The base tier lowers the cost of verifying authenticity for content consumers. The Select tier creates a premium signal for content that has passed expert review.

Whether the market will sustain this signal structure depends, as the organisation's own 2024 consultation process revealed, on whether the governance structure is sufficiently trusted by all the market participants it serves.

### 三、深度专访

### 3.1 TechCrunch 专访

发布日期：2025年1月20日

标题："Turing Certification CTO on building trustworthy AI detection at scale"

记者：Sarah Perez

专访对象：Dr. Nickolai Zeldovich，首席技术官（MIT CSAIL系统安全研究）

Q: 为什么选择区块链+AI的技术路线？

Dr. Zeldovich: The challenge has two separate components, and conflating them is what most earlier attempts got wrong. AI detection can tell you something about the likelihood that content was machine-generated — but it cannot tell you whether content has been tampered with after the fact, or whether the identity of the author is what it claims to be. Blockchain is very good at those second and third problems. Neither technology solves both problems alone. The architecture reflects that.

Q: AI检测误判率从18%降到6.3%是怎么做到的？

Dr. Zeldovich: Honestly? By acknowledging that a single model was the wrong architecture. When Ars Technica published their analysis in January 2024, the criticism was technically accurate. We had one model doing a job that required five. The Version 2.0 rebuild uses an ensemble of five independent detection models with a majority-vote consensus mechanism. Any content where confidence falls below 85% goes to mandatory human review. The improvement was not subtle.

Q: 5%以下的目标能实现吗？

Dr. Zeldovich: That is the target for 2025. The honest answer is that this is an arms race. As generative models become more sophisticated, detection becomes harder. The best we can commit to is continuous iteration, published quarterly performance data, and transparency when we fall short of targets.

### 3.3 Financial Times 专访

发布日期：2025年2月25日

标题："The woman building trust in the age of AI"

记者：Hannah Kuchler

专访对象：Dr. Claire Wardle，执行董事

关于双层认证体系的设计逻辑：

"We realised about a year in that provenance certification — what Turing Verified does — is necessary but not sufficient. Telling an audience that a piece of content has not been tampered with is very different from telling them it has been reviewed by domain experts and found to meet high standards. Those are two different trust claims, and conflating them would undermine both."

— Dr. Claire Wardle, Executive Director, Financial Times interview, February 2025

当被追问为何将两个层级设计成独立路径而非升级关系时，她进一步解释：

"We deliberately kept them separate. Turing Verified is the ticket into the digital trust ecosystem — it says: this content is what it claims to be. Turing Select is an additional credential for content creators to signal excellence above authenticity. The 850 reviewers don't judge whether content is true; they judge whether it is exceptional. That distinction keeps the semantics of each tier clean."

— Dr. Claire Wardle, 同上

技术深度摘录：

"The blockchain layer gets the most attention because it's the most legible technically. But the harder engineering problem is the privacy architecture. Journalists submitting content for certification may have sources whose identity must be protected. A system that requires full source disclosure as a condition of certification will not be adopted by the journalists who most need it. We use zero-knowledge proofs to prove that verification was performed correctly without exposing the underlying source material."

— Dr. Nickolai Zeldovich

发布日期：2024年1月（批评性），2025年3月（跟进报道）

标题（2025年3月跟进）："Turing Certification one year on: the false positive problem is better, not solved"

评测结论：

Version 2.0 delivered genuine improvement. The false positive rate is down from 18% to 6.3%. The zero-knowledge proof architecture is more carefully documented. The governance structure is meaningfully more representative. Our original criticism — that the technical claims outran the technical evidence — remains partially valid. The organisation has earned the acknowledgment that it listened and acted. It has not yet earned the claim that the detection problem is solved.
