### Statement

The Turing Trust (United Kingdom) and Turing Foundation (The Netherlands) are pleased to announce the formal establishment of Turing Certification — a global, multi-stakeholder certification system for digital information authenticity.

This announcement marks the beginning of a long-term institutional commitment to addressing one of the defining challenges of the AI era: the proliferation of synthetic and manipulated digital content that is eroding public trust in information.

### Why Now

The past three years have seen a dramatic acceleration in AI-generated content capabilities. Systems capable of producing convincing text, images, audio, and video — indistinguishable to many readers from human-created originals — are now widely accessible. The consequences are already visible: documented instances of AI-generated academic papers passing peer review, synthetic news articles circulating as genuine journalism, and deepfake video used in political contexts.

Existing responses — platform fact-checking, national legislation, voluntary industry standards — have proven fragmented and insufficient. A coordinated, global, standards-based response is necessary.

Turing Certification is our contribution to that response.

### What We Are Building

The Turing Certification system will comprise two certification tiers:

Turing Verified: A foundational certification confirming the provenance, integrity, and creator identity of digital content, implemented through blockchain-based distributed ledger technology.

Turing Select: An advanced certification tier that adds expert review of content quality and authority, for content with significant social impact. Turing Verified launches with the pilot programme in September 2023. Turing Select is planned for operational launch in 2024, following the establishment of the expert reviewer network and quality evaluation framework.

Both tiers are designed around principles of transparency, verifiability, and multi-stakeholder governance. No single institution — including our own — will have unilateral authority over certification outcomes.

### Governance Structure

Turing Certification will be governed through:

• A joint operational structure between The Turing Trust and Turing Foundation

• A Technical Advisory Committee drawing on expertise from leading global research institutions

• An Ethics Review Committee providing independent oversight of fairness and ethical conduct

• An Independent Audit Committee for financial and operational accountability

We are committed to ongoing expansion of this governance structure as the system matures, to ensure that it remains genuinely representative of the global communities it serves.

### Immediate Next Steps

Following this founding announcement, we will:

Q2 2023: Complete technical architecture build and begin internal testing Q2 2023: Finalize Certification Standards Version 1.0 Q3 2023: Launch the pilot certification program with a limited set of partner publishers Q4 2023: Complete pilot evaluation and publish results 2024: Begin broader rollout based on pilot learnings

We invite publishers, technology organizations, academic institutions, civil society groups, and individuals to engage with us as we build this system. Contact information for partnership inquiries is provided below.
